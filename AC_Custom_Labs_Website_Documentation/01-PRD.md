# AC CUSTOM LABS --- PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)

**Product:** AC Custom Labs Company Website\
**Brand:** AC Custom Labs\
**Purpose:** Technology services, software/app/web development, SEO and
digital solutions\
**Primary objective:** Generate qualified client enquiries and
communicate technical credibility.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Product Vision

AC Custom Labs needs a modern technology-company website that presents
the company as a capable digital product and engineering partner.

The supplied logo establishes the visual identity:

-   Black background
-   White `AC` mark
-   Red `Custom` wordmark
-   White `Labs`
-   Red curved underline/swoosh
-   Minimal geometric typography
-   High contrast
-   Technology/engineering feel

The website should extend this identity without becoming visually noisy.

### Brand personality

**Technical · Modern · Direct · Custom · Reliable · Conversion-focused**

## 8. Services Presentation

Each service should have:

-   Short title
-   One-line explanation
-   Deliverables
-   Technologies
-   Typical project outcome
-   CTA

Avoid promising specific business results unless supported by actual
project data.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 9. Portfolio

Portfolio cards:

-   Project image
-   Client/project name
-   Industry
-   Service
-   Technology
-   Short outcome
-   View case study

Case studies should follow:

``` text
Challenge
↓
Approach
↓
Solution
↓
Technology
↓
Outcome
```

Do not invent client names, metrics or results.

## 12. SEO Requirements

Target service-intent keywords around:

-   app development company
-   software development company
-   website development company
-   custom software development
-   SEO services
-   web development
-   SaaS development

Exact location targeting should only be added if the company wants local
SEO.

Implement: - Unique page titles - Meta descriptions - Canonicals - Open
Graph - Sitemap - Robots - Organization schema - Service schema where
appropriate

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 13. Accessibility

-   Semantic HTML
-   Keyboard navigation
-   Visible focus
-   Accessible forms
-   Proper contrast
-   Alt text
-   Reduced motion
-   Accessible mobile navigation

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 14. Performance

The website should be fast and lightweight.

Priorities: 1. Optimized images 2. Minimal client JavaScript 3. Static
generation where possible 4. Font optimization 5. Lazy loading 6. Avoid
unnecessary third-party embeds

## 16. Success Criteria

The website is ready when:

-   Brand identity is immediately recognizable.
-   Services are understandable.
-   Portfolio builds trust.
-   Primary CTA is obvious.
-   Mobile experience is excellent.
-   Contact/enquiry flow works.
-   No fabricated case studies or claims exist.
-   SEO foundations are implemented.
-   Performance is strong.
